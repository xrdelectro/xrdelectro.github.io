About xrd.lv

xrd.lv is a one-person workshop in Latvia that designs and sells DIY electronics kits. Everything is packed and shipped by the person who designed it.

Ordering

  • Browse and fill the cart without an account; you log in only to place the order.
  • Prices are in euro. The seller is not VAT-registered, so no VAT is added.
  • The checkout shows the total for each payment method before you order.
  • You get the invoice by email as soon as the order is placed.

Shipping

Orders ship from Latvia (EU) with Latvijas Pasts once the payment has arrived. You get the tracking number by email.

Contact

Write to e-mail, or reply to any order email. Installation guides and help are on each product's page.

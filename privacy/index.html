Privacy

What xrd.lv collects, why, who else sees it, how long it is kept, and your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Last updated: 14 November 2026.

Who is responsible

xrd.lv, a one-person business in Latvia; the seller's name and address are on every invoice. Contact for anything on this page: e-mail.

This site (www.xrd.lv)

No account, no cookies, no analytics or trackers. The pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web server, receives your IP address and browser details to deliver them (GitHub's privacy statement). Product pages ask the shop for current prices and stock; that request carries no personal data.

The installation guide embeds a YouTube video in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com): YouTube receives your IP address when the player loads, and sets cookies only once you play the video.

The shop (shop.xrd.lv)

Accountemail address, name, password (stored only as an argon2 hash), or the identity Google or another sign-in service vouches for (an account number and your verified email address); when you joined, last signed in and last visited
Orderscontact and delivery details (name, address, phone, email, optional company and VAT number), what you ordered, notes you add, payment method, payment confirmations and references, tracking numbers
Browserone cookie that keeps you signed in, and one for a few minutes while signing in through another service; the cart stays in your browser's storage (never sent until you order)
Server logsIP address, time, address requested and browser; in full (including cookies) for requests that look like attacks or fail

Why, and on what legal basis

  • To sell and deliver what you order, send the invoice and order emails, and answer you: needed for the contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Keeping invoices and payment records for as long as accounting law requires: legal obligation (art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Security: server logs, sign-in records and limits against abuse: legitimate interest in keeping the shop and your account safe (art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Statistics for the shop owner (number of accounts and orders, last visit): legitimate interest in running the shop; nothing is shared and nothing profiles you.

No newsletters or marketing: your address is used only for your orders and your account.

Who else receives data

  • Latvijas Pasts (Latvia): name, delivery address, phone and email, to deliver the parcel and tell you about it.
  • AhaSend (EU): sends the shop's emails, so it handles your address and the message.
  • Google, or another sign-in service, only if you choose to sign in with it.
  • Your bank and ours: a bank transfer shows the payer's name and the reference you use.
  • Card or PayPal payments, if offered and chosen: Stripe or PayPal handle the payment on their own pages; the shop receives only the result.

The shop runs on its own server in Latvia; nothing is sold or given to anyone else. Google and GitHub may process data outside the EU under their own safeguards (EU standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework).

How long

  • Server logs: 30 days.
  • Orders and invoices: as long as accounting law requires.
  • Accounts: until you ask for deletion; then the account is removed and only what accounting law requires is kept.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, a limit on its use, or a copy in a machine-readable form, and you may object to uses based on legitimate interest. Write to e-mail from the address of your account; you get an answer within a month. You may also complain to the Latvian data protection authority, Datu valsts inspekcija.
